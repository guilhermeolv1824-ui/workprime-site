About Work Prime

Shore builders trust us with their framing. You can too.

Most of our work comes from Jersey Shore builders who hand us the structure of their homes: walls, floors, roof systems and decks, from the ground up. Homes in Cape May, Wildwood and Atlantic City stand on framing we built.

We now take on a limited number of projects directly for homeowners, starting at $3,000: additions, second stories, custom decks, garages and new home framing. You get the same crew and the same standard the builders count on.

What We Build

Big structural projects. Built right.

We focus on additions, decks and new construction. Every project starts at $3,000.

01

Home Additions & Second Stories

Add the space without moving. We frame ground-level and second-story additions, tied cleanly into your existing structure and built to code.

  • Room & primary suite additions
  • Second-story additions
  • Raise-up framing for elevated homes
  • Structural tie-ins to existing homes
02

Custom Shore Decks

Multi-level decks, roof decks and wraparounds built for salt air and coastal wind. Reinforced framing, wood or composite, with custom railings.

  • Multi-level & wraparound decks
  • Rooftop decks
  • Wood & composite decking
  • Custom railing systems
03

New Home Framing

Whole-house framing from sill plate to ridge: the same packages Shore builders hire us for.

  • Whole-house framing packages
  • Wall, floor & roof systems
  • Roof truss & rafter framing
  • Structural shells
04

Garages & Pool Houses

Detached structures framed with the same care as the main house.

  • Detached garages
  • Pool houses & cabanas
  • Carriage houses & accessory buildings
05

Structural Renovations

Open up a closed floor plan or change how a house lives, with the beams and headers to carry it.

  • Load-bearing wall removal & beams
  • Open floor plan conversions
  • Full-house structural renovations

What's your minimum project size?

Where do you work?

Up and down the Jersey Shore (Cape May, the Wildwoods, Stone Harbor, Avalon, Sea Isle, Ocean City, Margate, Ventnor, Longport, Atlantic City and Brigantine), plus Burlington County.
